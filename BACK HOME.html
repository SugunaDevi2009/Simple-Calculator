SIMPLE CALCULATOR

A simple calculator is a compact electronic device designed to perform everyday, basic arithmetic. It typically features an 8 to 12-digit display screen and a straightforward keypad containing numbers, a decimal point, and the four core operation buttons (addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division). Many models also include a few convenient extras, such as a percentage key, a square root function, and basic memory storage (M+/M-). Because it relies on a dual-power system of a small solar panel and a backup battery, it remains highly reliable and accessible for quick, everyday math at school, home, or the office.
